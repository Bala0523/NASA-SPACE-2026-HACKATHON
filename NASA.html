About Our Project

Abandoned but Not Forgotten is an interactive digital storytelling platform that explores the history of abandoned space equipment on the Moon and Mars. Our project helps students understand the purpose, history and importance of space missions. Using HTML, CSS, JavaScript and 3D visualization technologies, we transform forgotten space objects into interesting educational stories. Our mission is to remind everyone that even when a spacecraft stops working, its contribution to science is never forgotten.
